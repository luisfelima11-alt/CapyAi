Grammar: have, has e a armadilha do does

Um verbo, duas formas — e uma pegadinha que aparece em toda anamnese: na pergunta e na negativa, o has volta a ser have.

I / you / we / they → have
he / she / it → has

✅ I have two medicines.
✅ My mother has high blood pressure.
✅ Dr. Rigolin has the old file. (um nome = he/she)

❌ My mother have it.
Esta é a pegadinha da aula. O -s já está no does, então o verbo volta à forma pura:

✅ Does anyone in your family have it?
❌ Does anyone haves it?

✅ He doesn't have allergies.
❌ He doesn't has allergies.

Com I / you / we / they é do e don't:
✅ Do you have any allergies? / I don't have any.
any aparece em pergunta e negativa; some em afirmativa:

✅ Do you have any allergies?
✅ I don't have any medicine at home.
✅ I take some medicine for my blood pressure.

Depois de any, o substantivo contável vai no plural: any allergies, any questions.

⚠️ Em oferta, some também entra em pergunta: Would you like some water?
Para perguntar se algo aconteceu alguma vez, use have + ever + particípio:

✅ Have you ever had surgery? (had = particípio de have)
✅ Have you ever been in hospital?

❌ Have you ever have surgery?

Resposta curta: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. — nunca Yes, I had.

Grammar: The 4 Past Tenses Together

Referência rápida, árvore de decisão e como combinar todos os tempos numa narrativa natural.

PAST SIMPLE: completed action, specific time → "I went to Paris in 2022."
PAST CONTINUOUS: action in progress at a past moment → "I was sleeping at 8am."
PRESENT PERFECT: experience / result still relevant now → "I have visited Paris." / "She has just arrived."
PAST PERFECT: action completed BEFORE another past action → "I had eaten before she arrived."
📐 Sinais: yesterday/ago/last = Past Simple · while/when (em progresso) = Past Continuous · ever/never/just/already/yet = Present Perfect · by the time/before/after = Past Perfect.
Action completed? → Specific time mentioned? YES → Past Simple · NO → Present Perfect.
Action ongoing/in progress? → In the past → Past Continuous.
Happened BEFORE another past event? → Past Perfect.
⚠️ Erro comum: "Yesterday I have gone" ❌ → "Yesterday I went" ✅ (tempo específico = Past Simple, nunca Present Perfect).
Histórias naturais combinam os tempos: "I had been working (Past Perfect) at the company for 5 years when one morning, while I was walking (Past Continuous) to the office, I received (Past Simple) a call that changed everything."
"Since then, my life has never been (Present Perfect) the same."
📐 Dica: conte uma história usando ao menos 3 tempos passados diferentes. Cada um adiciona uma camada de significado: o que aconteceu, o que estava acontecendo, o que já tinha acontecido antes, e o que ainda importa agora.

Grammar: Job Interview English

STAR method, confident language for strengths, and handling tricky questions.

Situation: Set the scene briefly.
Task: What was your responsibility?
Action: What did YOU specifically do?
Result: What was the outcome?
📐 Example: "Tell me about a time you solved a problem." → S: "Our main client threatened to leave." T: "I was responsible for client relations." A: "I arranged an emergency meeting and proposed a new service package." R: "We retained the client and grew the account by 20%."
"I am particularly strong in..."
"One of my key strengths is..."
"I've always been good at..."
"I tend to excel when..."
💡 Tip: Always support a strength with a real example — "I'm good at X — for example, in my last role I..."
"What's your greatest weakness?" → Turn it into a growth story: "I used to struggle with [X], but I've been actively working on it by [action], and I've seen real improvement."
"Why did you leave your last job?" → Focus on what you're moving TOWARDS, not away from: "I'm looking for a role where I can [positive goal]."

Grammar: Past Continuous

Estrutura, contraste com Past Simple e uso narrativo.

Positive: "I was studying." / "They were working."
Negative: "She wasn't sleeping." / "We weren't paying attention."
Question: "Were you travelling alone?" / "What was he doing?"
📐 Regra: Was para I/He/She/It; Were para You/We/They.
Past Continuous = background/ongoing action (longer).
Past Simple = sudden/completed action (shorter, interrupts).
Pattern: "I WAS STUDYING (continuous) WHEN my phone RANG (simple)."
"While she WAS COOKING (continuous), he WAS CLEANING (continuous)."
📐 Regra: WHEN + Past Simple geralmente interrompe um Past Continuous.
Use Past Continuous to paint a picture before the main event.
"It was a stormy night. The wind WAS HOWLING, the fire WAS CRACKLING, and I WAS READING a mystery novel, when suddenly... I HEARD footsteps."
🎭 Dica: This is how great storytellers build atmosphere and engage listeners.

Grammar: Present Perfect

Estrutura, advérbios-chave e contraste com Past Simple.

Positive: "I have visited." / "She has eaten."
Negative: "We haven't finished." / "He hasn't called."
Question: "Have you been there?" / "Has she eaten?"
Common irregular past participles: gone, seen, eaten, been, done, had, known, spoken, taken, written.
💡 Tip: The past participle of irregular verbs is the THIRD column in verb tables.
EVER (experience questions): "Have you EVER...?"
NEVER (negative experience): "I've NEVER..."
ALREADY (done before expected): "I've ALREADY done it."
YET (not done, expected): "I haven't done it YET." / "Have you done it YET?"
JUST (very recently): "I've JUST finished."
FOR / SINCE (duration): "for 3 years" / "since 2020"
Use Present Perfect when NO specific time is mentioned — it connects the past to NOW.
Use Past Simple when a SPECIFIC time is mentioned.
"I have visited Paris." (life experience, anytime) vs "I visited Paris in 2019." (specific time)
"I've eaten sushi." ✅ vs "I've eaten sushi last Tuesday." ❌ → "I ate sushi last Tuesday." ✅
📐 Regra: The moment you say WHEN → switch to Past Simple.

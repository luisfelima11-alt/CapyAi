Past Continuous

Used to describe actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past.

📌 Structure

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

✔ Affirmative
I was working
✘ Negative
She wasn't sleeping
? Question
Were they coming?
At 8 PM, I was watching TV. (specific time)
They were having dinner when I called. (interrupted)
While she was reading, he was cooking.
I was listening to music while I was studying.
The sun was shining and birds were singing.
The rain was falling when we arrived at the park.

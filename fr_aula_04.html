📐 Grammar

Possessive adjectives — my, your, his/her in French.

Possessive Adjectives

MY

mon (M): mon père

ma (F): ma mère

mes (pl): mes parents

YOUR

ton (M): ton frère

ta (F): ta sœur

tes (pl): tes amis

HIS/HER

son (M): son fils

sa (F): sa fille

ses (pl): ses enfants

In French, mon/ma/mes depend on the noun's gender, NOT the speaker's gender:

✅ A woman says: mon père (my father) — père is M, so use MON

✅ A man says: ma mère (my mother) — mère is F, so use MA

💡 This confuses English speakers! Always think "what gender is the NOUN?" not "who is speaking?"

Use mon (not ma) before feminine nouns starting with a vowel:

✅ mon amie (my friend — F, starts with vowel)

✅ mon école (my school — F, starts with vowel)

💡 This prevents the awkward "ma amie" sound — French loves smooth pronunciation!

For "our", "your (formal/plural)", "their":

✅ notre père / notre mère — our father / mother

✅ votre famille — your family (formal)

✅ leur fils / leurs enfants — their son / their children

Use avoir (to have) when describing family:

✅ J'ai un frère et deux sœurs. — I have a brother and two sisters.

✅ Elle a trois enfants. — She has three children.

✅ Il est fils unique. — He is an only child. (use être for "being")

🎭 Dialogue

🏠 Yara introduces her family to Leo. Read aloud!

🏠 Yara shows Leo a family photo. She introduces each family member using possessives!

🐾 Yara

Leo, regarde! C'est ma famille.

Leo, look! This is my family.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Oh, c'est qui, là?

Oh, who is that there?

🐾 Yara

Ici, c'est mon père et ma mère. Et là, c'est mon frère Carlos.

Here, that's my father and my mother. And there, that's my brother Carlos.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Et tu as des sœurs?

And do you have any sisters?

🐾 Yara

Oui! J'ai une sœur, Sofia. Et voilà mes grands-parents!

Yes! I have one sister, Sofia. And here are my grandparents!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Quelle belle famille! Et toi, tu es fille unique?

What a beautiful family! And you, are you an only child?

🐾 Yara

Non! J'ai un frère et une sœur. Et mon oncle et ma tante habitent aussi à Paris!

No! I have a brother and a sister. And my uncle and my aunt also live in Paris!

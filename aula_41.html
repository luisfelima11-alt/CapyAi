📐 Grammar

Como usar WHO, WHICH, THAT, WHERE e WHOSE corretamente.

Relative Pronouns at a Glance

WHO

for people

The teacher who inspired me

WHICH

for things / animals

The book which I read

THAT

people or things (informal)

The movie that won the prize

WHERE

for places

The city where I was born

WHOSE

possession (of whom / of which)

The student whose essay won

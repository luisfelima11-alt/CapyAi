📐 Grammar

HAVE, WANT, NEED & LIKE no Present Simple.

Os 4 Verbos Essenciais

HAVE ❤️ WANT ⭐ NEED 🔑 LIKE 😊

I / You / We / They → have  |  He / She / It → has

✅ I have a dog. → Eu tenho um cachorro.

✅ She has a great idea. → Ela tem uma ótima ideia.

✅ Do you have time? → Você tem tempo?

I / You / We / They → want  |  He / She / It → wants

✅ I want to learn English. → Quero aprender inglês.

✅ She wants a new phone. → Ela quer um celular novo.

✅ What do you want? → O que você quer?

I / You / We / They → need  |  He / She / It → needs

✅ I need help. → Preciso de ajuda.

✅ She needs more practice. → Ela precisa de mais prática.

✅ Do you need anything? → Precisa de alguma coisa?

I / You / We / They → like  |  He / She / It → likes

✅ I like music. → Gosto de música.

✅ He likes to read. → Ele gosta de ler.

✅ Do you like English? → Você gosta de inglês?

Na 3ª pessoa do singular (he/she/it) sempre adicione -S!

✅ He has / She wants / It needs / He likes

❌ He have / She want / It need

💡 Só HAVE muda para HAS — os outros só adicionam -S!

🎭 Dialogue

🌿 Cabana de Nano Banana. Leia em voz alta.

🌿 Cabana de Nano Banana. Leo chega pela primeira vez e encontra Capy Yara. É o começo de uma grande aventura!

🐾 Capy Yara

Hi Leo! Welcome to Nano Banana! Do you have everything you need for our adventure?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Hi Yara! I have my notebook. I need to learn English — I really want to speak fluently!

🐾 Capy Yara

That's great! I like students who are motivated. What kind of things do you like to talk about?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I like music, travel, and food. I also have a dog — his name is Bolt! He needs a walk every day.

🐾 Capy Yara

I love dogs! Bolt has a great owner. You have everything — motivation, energy, and a plan. Let's start! 🚀

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Yes! I want to practice every day. I need your help, Yara. I like this place already! 😊

Verbos de Comunicação e Emoção

TELL ✉️ WORK 💼 FEEL ❤️ LEAVE 🚪

I/You/We/They tell | He/She/It tells

✅ Tell me more! → Me conta mais!

✅ She tells great stories. → Ela conta ótimas histórias.

💡 TELL sempre precisa de um objeto (pessoa): tell ME, tell HIM, tell the truth

I/You/We/They work | He/She/It works

✅ She works from home. → Ela trabalha de casa.

✅ This plan works perfectly. → Esse plano funciona perfeitamente.

✅ Hard work pays off. → Trabalho duro compensa.

I/You/We/They feel | He/She/It feels

✅ How do you feel? → Como você está se sentindo?

✅ I feel like pizza tonight! → Estou com vontade de pizza hoje!

✅ She feels confident now. → Ela se sente confiante agora.

I/You/We/They leave | He/She/It leaves

✅ Leave it to me! → Deixa comigo!

✅ I leave work at 6pm. → Saio do trabalho às 18h.

✅ Don't leave me! → Não me deixe!

🎭 Dialogue

📚 Sala de Estudos de Nano Banana. Leia em voz alta.

📚 Sala de Estudos de Nano Banana. Leo se sente nervoso antes do primeiro teste e Yara o apoia com palavras e histórias.

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo, how do you feel today? You left early yesterday.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I feel nervous. Can I tell you something? I'm scared to work hard and still fail.

🐾 Capy Yara

Tell me everything! That feeling is normal. Let me tell you a story...

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Please! I need to hear it. I don't want to leave this course — I feel like it's working!

🐾 Capy Yara

It IS working! Every student who feels nervous at first is the one who succeeds later. Don't leave. Leave it to me — we work through this together! 💪

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Thank you, Yara. I feel so much better! I'm going to tell myself every day: "Work hard and it will pay off!" 🌟

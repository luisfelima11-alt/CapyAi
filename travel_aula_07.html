Grammar: Renting a Car

Modal verbs, Conditional Type 1 e question tags para locação de veículos.

"You must have a valid driving licence." → Obrigação.
"You can't park here." → Proibição.
"You should check the insurance coverage." → Recomendação.
📐 Regra: modal + base verb (sem -s, sem -ing). Ex: "You must have", não "You must to have".
"If you return it late, they will charge you extra."
"If you damage the car, the insurance will cover it."
"If there is no GPS, I'll use my phone."
📐 Regra: If + present simple → will + base verb. Situações reais e prováveis no futuro.
"You need a credit card, don't you?"
"The tank is full, isn't it?"
"I can drive in Europe with my licence, can't I?"
📐 Regra: Afirmativo → tag negativa; Negativo → tag positiva. Usadas para confirmar informações.

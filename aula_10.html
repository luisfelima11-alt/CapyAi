📐 Grammar

A regra dos contáveis e incontáveis — a chave para tudo!

A Regra Fundamental

🔢 MANY + contável (countable) → many books, many people, many times
💧 MUCH + incontável (uncountable) → much water, much time, much money
✅ A LOT OF + ambos → a lot of books / a lot of water

A distinção mais importante da língua inglesa para quantificadores!

Contáveis (Countable): têm plural → 1 book, 2 books, 3 apples

Incontáveis (Uncountable): sem plural → water, money, information, advice, news

⚠️ Atenção: em português muitas palavras são contáveis, mas em inglês não! Ex: "uma informação" → "some information" (nunca: "an information")

Use MANY principalmente em perguntas e negativas:

❓ How many students are in the class?

❌ I don't have many friends in the city.

✅ There are many options. (afirmativa também é possível, mas mais formal)

Use MUCH com substantivos incontáveis:

❓ How much water do you drink per day?

❌ I don't have much time right now — maybe later.

❌ There isn't much money in my account this month!

A LOT OF funciona com contáveis E incontáveis — perfeito para afirmativas!

✅ She has a lot of friends. (contável)

✅ I drank a lot of water today. (incontável)

✅ We have lots of options — which one do you prefer?

Outros quantificadores essenciais:

SOME → afirmativas e ofertas → I have some coffee. / Would you like some?

ANY → perguntas e negativas → Do you have any milk? / I don't have any plans.

A FEW → poucos (contável, positivo) → I have a few minutes.

FEW → quase nenhum (contável, negativo) → Few people know this secret.

A LITTLE → um pouco (incontável, positivo) → I speak a little Spanish.

ENOUGH → suficiente → I have enough money for the trip.

Para comparar e exagerar!

MORE + noun → I want more coffee. / I need more time.

THE MORE... THE MORE → The more you practice, the better you get!

TOO MANY + contável → There are too many people here.

TOO MUCH + incontável → I drank too much coffee — I can't sleep!

Leo, do we have enough fruit for the week? I think we need to buy some more.

Well, there are a few bananas left, but not many. And we don't have any mangoes at all!

🐾 Capy Yara

Oh no! How much money do we have? We can't spend too much today — there's a lot of stuff we still need for the house.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

We have some money left — about 30 coins. There aren't many options, but we can get a little of everything.

🐾 Capy Yara

Perfect! The more variety we have, the better our meals will be. Also, how many lessons have you done this week, Leo?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

A lot of them! There's so much to learn, but honestly — the more I study, the more I love it! 🌟

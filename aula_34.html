Grammar: Opinions & Debate

How to give, agree with, disagree with, and balance opinions politely.

"In my opinion, Katy Perry is one of the most creative artists in pop."
"Personally, I think Elite has more original storytelling than most American series."
"I strongly believe that consistent marketing is more powerful than viral moments."
"I couldn't agree more — digital marketing is essential today."
"That's a fair point about House of the Dragon's production quality."
"Exactly! You have a really valid point."
"I beg to differ — James Arthur's emotional impact is stronger."
"With all due respect, I think Friends holds up better than people say."
"Actually, I see it a bit differently — let me explain."
"You have a point, but I still think GoT early seasons were better."
"That's true, however, the marketing strategy for 1D was unique."
"I see where you're coming from, but I still prefer Elite."

🎯 Hot Take Challenge

Katy Perry · One Direction · Adele · Marketing Digital

🔥

What is a "Hot Take"?

A hot take is a bold, confident opinion — sometimes controversial — that you state and defend. It's a great way to practice expressing and defending your views in English!

🎤
"One Direction had a better digital marketing strategy than Katy Perry in their peak years."
Do you agree or disagree? Write 3 sentences defending your position.
Starters: "In my opinion..." / "I beg to differ because..." / "That's a fair point, but..."
📱
"Adele doesn't need social media — her music sells itself. Most artists should follow her approach."
What's your stance? Write 3 sentences.
Starters: "I strongly believe..." / "I see where you're coming from, but..." / "Actually, I think..."
🚀
"For a marketing professional in Brazil, learning English is more important than any digital tool."
This one is personal! Write 3 sentences defending your view.
Starters: "From my perspective..." / "That's a valid point because..." / "I couldn't agree more because..."
🗣️ Remember to use:
In my opinion... I strongly believe... That's a fair point... I beg to differ... You have a point, but... Let's agree to disagree!

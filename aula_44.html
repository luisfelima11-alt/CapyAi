3. Error Correction

Find and fix the grammar mistake in each sentence.

❌ "I have worked there since three years."

💡 Hint: since → for (use "for" with periods of time: for three years, for months)

❌ "Have you ever went to an international conference?"

💡 Hint: went → gone (irregular Past Participle of go: go → went → gone)

❌ "I'm going to brought new ideas to the team."

💡 Hint: going to brought → going to bring (going to + bare infinitive, not past tense)

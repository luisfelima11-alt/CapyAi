📐 Grammar

A estrutura do Present Perfect e suas palavras-chave.

Estrutura

Subject + have / has + Past Participle

• I / You / We / They → have

• He / She / It → has

Usado em perguntas para perguntar sobre experiências de vida.

✅ Have you ever tried sushi? (Você já provou sushi alguma vez?)

✅ Has she ever visited Japan? (Ela já visitou o Japão?)

Resposta negativa a uma experiência. Não precisa de "not".

✅ I have never tried surfing. (Nunca tentei fazer surf.)

✅ They have never visited the south. (Eles nunca visitaram o sul.)

Algo aconteceu antes do esperado. Vai entre have e o particípio.

✅ I have already finished the lesson. (Já terminei a lição.)

✅ She has already eaten. (Ela já comeu.)

Algo aconteceu há pouquíssimo tempo, agora mesmo.

✅ He has just arrived. (Ele acabou de chegar.)

✅ I have just tried a new approach. (Acabei de tentar uma abordagem nova.)

Em negativas = "ainda não". Em perguntas = "já?". Sempre no final da frase.

✅ She hasn't called yet. (Ela ainda não ligou.)

✅ Have you finished yet? (Você já terminou?)

Se você consegue dizer QUANDO → Simple Past
Se é experiência sem data → Present Perfect

❌ "I have seen him yesterday."

✅ "I saw him yesterday." (data específica)

❌ "I have went there."

✅ "I have been there." (been, não went)

📚 Biblioteca Mágica de Nano Banana. Leo encontra Capy Yara lendo um livro enorme. As páginas brilham com letras douradas que flutuam pelo ar.

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo! Have you ever tried reading in English? This magic book holds all the adventures you have already lived!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Have I? I have never thought about it. But I have already seen some English books!

🐾 Capy Yara

Exactly! The Present Perfect connects your past to NOW. You have learned so much already — have you noticed?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I have just realized that! I have changed a lot since we started. I have never given up!

🐾 Capy Yara

That's the spirit! Have you ever visited the Crystal Tower? You have grown enough to try!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I have not been there yet — but I'm ready. Let's go, Yara! 🚀

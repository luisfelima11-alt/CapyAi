Grammar: Hedging & Politeness

Como suavizar com modais, distanciar opiniões e fazer pedidos indiretos.

Pedido direto → suave: "Give me a hand." → "Could you give me a hand?"
Opinião → suave: "You are wrong." → "I would say that is not quite right."
Sugestão → suave: "Do it again." → "You might want to try it again."
📐 Regra: o passado do modal (could/would/might) soa mais distante e polido que o presente (can/will/may) — mesmo falando do presente.
Abridores de hedge: "I would say...", "It seems that...", "Perhaps...", "As far as I know...".
Impessoal (distancia a crítica): "You made a mistake." → "There seems to be a mistake here."
Verbos de hedge: "I tend to think...", "I am not entirely sure, but..." — sinalizam abertura à discordância.
Pedido indireto (embedded): "Where is the file?" → "Would you mind telling me where the file is?" / "I was wondering if you could help."
Downtoners suavizam o negativo: "It is late." → "It is a bit / rather / slightly late." / "I kind of disagree."
Understatement (típico britânico): "It was terrible." → "It wasn't ideal." / "That's not bad" = na verdade, muito bom.

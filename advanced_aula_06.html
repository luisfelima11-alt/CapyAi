Grammar: Advanced Reported Speech

Padrões de verbos, recuo verbal (backshift) e como reportar perguntas e ordens.

+ that-clause — claim, insist, admit, deny, imply, complain: "He claimed that he had finished."
+ obj + to-inf — tell, ask, advise, warn, urge, beg, remind: "She told me to call her back."
+ gerund (-ing) — admit, deny, suggest, recommend, regret: "He admitted breaking the vase."
📐 Regra: alguns verbos aceitam mais de um padrão. Negativo do to-inf = not to do (não "to not do"): "She warned me not to go."
Present Simple → Past Simple: "I work" → He said he worked.
Will → Would · Can → Could · May → Might · Must → Had to: "I will help" → She said she would help.
NÃO recue quando: (a) o relato é presente ("He says he is tired"), ou (b) é uma verdade geral ("She said water boils at 100°C"). Could/would/should/might também não recuam.
Reported Wh-question: ordem AFIRMATIVA, sem do/does/did. "Where do you live?" → He asked me where I lived.
Reported Yes/No: use if/whether. "Are you ready?" → She asked whether I was ready.
Reported commands: verb + obj + (not) to-inf. "Sit down!" → He told us to sit down. "Do not be late!" → She warned us not to be late.

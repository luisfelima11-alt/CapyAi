📐 Grammar

Color adjectives must agree in gender and number with the noun.

The Gender Agreement Rule

Masculine ♂

un pull rouge — a red sweater

un chien noir — a black dog

Feminine ♀

une robe rouge — a red dress

une chatte noire — a black cat

Most colors add -e in feminine form (if they don't already end in -e):

✅ noir → noire (black)

✅ vert → verte (green)

✅ violet → violette (purple — doubles consonant!)

✅ rouge → rouge (red — already ends in -e, no change!)

✅ bleu → bleue (blue)

Some colors are invariable — they never change!

✅ orange stays orange for M and F

✅ marron (brown) stays marron

💡 These are usually colors that come from nouns (orange = the fruit, marron = the chestnut).

Unlike English, color adjectives come AFTER the noun:

✅ une voiture rouge = a red car (lit. "a car red")

✅ un chat blanc = a white cat

❌ NOT: une rouge voiture

For plural nouns, add -s to the color adjective:

✅ des robes rouges (red dresses — plural F)

✅ des chiens noirs (black dogs — plural M)

✅ des chapeaux verts (green hats — plural M)

🎭 Dialogue

🛍️ Shopping in a French boutique. Read aloud!

🛍️ Yara is shopping for clothes in a Parisian boutique. She needs to know the colors!

🐾 Yara

Bonjour! Je cherche une robe. Vous avez cette robe en bleu?

Hello! I'm looking for a dress. Do you have this dress in blue?

🧑‍💼 Vendeur

Oui! Nous avons en bleu, rouge et noir.

Yes! We have it in blue, red, and black.

🐾 Yara

Parfait! J'adore la robe bleue. Et ce pull, il est en quelle couleur?

Perfect! I love the blue dress. And this sweater, what color is it?

🧑‍💼 Vendeur

C'est un pull vert. Très à la mode cette saison!

It's a green sweater. Very fashionable this season!

🐾 Yara

Hmm, j'aime le vert, mais je préfère le blanc. Vous avez en blanc?

Hmm, I like green, but I prefer white. Do you have it in white?

🧑‍💼 Vendeur

Bien sûr! Un pull blanc, voilà! Vous aimez?

Of course! A white sweater, here you go! Do you like it?

🐾 Yara

Oui, magnifique! Une robe bleue et un pull blanc — les couleurs du drapeau français! 🇫🇷

Yes, magnificent! A blue dress and a white sweater — the colors of the French flag!

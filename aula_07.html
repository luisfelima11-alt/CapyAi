📐 Grammar

Past Simple com verbos regulares.

A Fórmula

✅ Afirmativa: sujeito + verbo-ED → I walked / She worked
❌ Negativa: sujeito + didn't + verbo base → I didn't walk
❓ Pergunta: Did + sujeito + verbo base? → Did she work?

A maioria dos verbos: apenas adicione -ED

walk → walked | talk → talked | play → played

work → worked | listen → listened | watch → watched

Atenção às exceções!

🔸 Termina em -E: só adicione D → like → liked | love → loved

🔸 Termina em consoante + Y: Y → IED → study → studied | try → tried

🔸 CVC curto: dobra a consoante → stop → stopped | plan → planned

Na negativa, use didn't + verbo no infinitivo!

✅ I didn't walk (não: I didn't walked)

✅ She didn't study → Ela não estudou

✅ We didn't work yesterday → Não trabalhamos ontem

Na pergunta, use Did + sujeito + verbo base!

✅ Did you study last night? → Você estudou ontem à noite?

✅ Did she call? → Ela ligou?

✅ Did they play? → Eles jogaram?

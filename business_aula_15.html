Grammar: Diplomatic & Intercultural Language

Estruturas gramaticais para comunicar diferenças culturais com tato e respeito em inglês profissional.

"It seems to me that there might be a cultural difference in how we approach hierarchy."
"I may be wrong, but I sense there's been a misunderstanding about the etiquette."
"If I understand correctly, the custom here is to reach consensus before deciding."
💡 Dica: Linguagem diplomática usa hedges como "it seems", "I may be wrong", "if I understand" para comunicar pontos sensíveis sem ser direto ou ofensivo.
"In my culture, we tend to be more indirect when giving feedback."
"One difference I've noticed is that formality levels vary greatly between our teams."
"I find that approaches to hierarchy can differ significantly across cultures."
⚡ Regra: Use "tend to" para generalizações — é mais preciso e menos julgamental do que "always" ou "never". "I find that" é suave e observacional.
"She said that in Japan, exchanging business cards is a formal ritual."
"He mentioned that the team prefers reaching consensus before making decisions."
"They explained that indirect communication is a sign of respect, not evasion."
🗣️ Regra: Reported speech (said that / mentioned that) permite compartilhar perspectivas culturais de outros sem parecer que você está impondo sua opinião.

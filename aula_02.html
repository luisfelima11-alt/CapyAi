Verbos de Ação

GO 🚶 DO ✅ SAY 💬 SEE 👁️

I/You/We/They go | He/She/It goes

✅ I go to school every day. → Vou à escola todos os dias.

✅ She goes to the gym on Mondays. → Ela vai à academia nas segundas.

✅ Let's go! → Vamos!

💡 GO + -ING: go swimming, go shopping, go running

I/You/We/They do | He/She/It does

✅ I do my homework every night. → Faço minha lição toda noite.

✅ She does yoga in the morning. → Ela faz yoga de manhã.

✅ What do you do? → O que você faz? (profissão)

I/You/We/They say | He/She/It says

✅ What did you say? → O que você disse?

✅ She says hello to everyone. → Ela diz olá para todos.

💡 SAY + something. TELL + someone: I say "hello." / I tell you a secret.

I/You/We/They see | He/She/It sees

✅ I see what you mean. → Entendo o que você quer dizer.

✅ Can you see the sign? → Você consegue ver a placa?

✅ See you later! → Até mais!

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo, look! Do you see that beautiful waterfall? I go there every morning!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Wow! I see it! It's amazing! What do you do there?

🐾 Capy Yara

I do my morning exercises and practice English. I say new words out loud — it really helps!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

That's a great idea! What do you say exactly? How does it work?

🐾 Capy Yara

I say the word, the translation, and then a sentence. Then I go to the next word. It's simple but powerful!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I see what you mean! Let's go together tomorrow — I want to do this too! 🌊

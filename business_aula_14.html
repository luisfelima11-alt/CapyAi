Grammar: Financial Language

Estruturas gramaticais para planejamento financeiro e comparação de opções em inglês profissional.

"If we cut costs by 10%, we'll meet our revenue target this quarter."
"If the invoice isn't paid by Friday, we'll have a cashflow deficit."
"If we reallocate the surplus, we can fund the new project."
💡 Regra: First Conditional (If + Present Simple → will) para cenários financeiros realistas e prováveis no planejamento.
"Option A is more cost-effective, whereas Option B has a higher profit margin."
"Compared to last year, our overhead has decreased significantly."
"Although Option B costs more upfront, the ROI is considerably higher."
⚡ Conectivos: whereas / while (contraste), compared to (comparação), although / even though (concessão). Essenciais para relatórios financeiros.
"Our annual revenue is $2.5 million — up from $1.8 million last fiscal year."
"One-third of the budget allocation goes to overhead costs."
"We're targeting a 15% profit margin by the end of the fiscal year."
🗣️ Dica: Em inglês: million (6 zeros), billion (9 zeros). Frações: half, one-third, one-quarter, two-thirds. Nunca diga "milliard".

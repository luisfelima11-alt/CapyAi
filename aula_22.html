The 3 Uses of MOST

MOST is one of the most useful words in English — but it works differently in each context.

📌 Quick Overview

Use 1
THE most → superlative
Use 2
Most + noun → majority
Use 3
MOST = maximum degree
This is the most interesting book I've ever read.
She is the most creative person on the team.
Brazil has the most biodiversity in the world.

📝 THE is required before "most" in superlatives. Short adjectives use -EST: tallest, fastest.

Most people want to be happy. (in general)
Most countries have their own language.
Most of the time, I prefer to cook at home.

📝 Most of the + specific group: "Most of the students passed." | Most + general: "Most students like music."

What do you enjoy most? (= most of all)
I like travelling most when the weather is good.
She worries most about her family.

📝 In this use, MOST comes after the verb and has no article. It means "above everything else".

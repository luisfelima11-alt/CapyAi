Grammar: comparativos (cheaper / more expensive)

Um único ponto: comparar preços e opções. É o que o cliente sempre pergunta.

"This antenna is cheaper than the other one."
"The new part is faster to install."
"This repair is easier than the last one."
💡 Regra: Adjetivo curto ganha -er + than: cheap → cheaper than.
"The original part is more expensive than the copy."
"This model is more reliable."
"Repairing is cheaper than buying a new one."
⚡ Dica: Adjetivo com 3+ sílabas usa more: more expensive, more reliable.

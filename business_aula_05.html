Grammar: Small Talk Language

Estruturas gramaticais para conversas informais no ambiente profissional.

"It's been a busy week, hasn't it?"
"You went to the conference, didn't you?"
"The weather is amazing today, isn't it?"
💡 Regra: Frase positiva + tag negativa / Frase negativa + tag positiva.
"Have you heard about the merger?" — notícia recente
"Have you seen the new office renovation?" — algo novo
"I've just started using the new system." — ação recente
⚡ Dica: Present Perfect (have + past participle) = experiências ou notícias recentes sem tempo definido.
"I personally think the new system is much better."
"Actually, I prefer working from home on Fridays."
"To be honest, the commute has been tough lately."
🗣️ Dica: Softeners tornam a conversa mais natural e menos direta — fundamentais para small talk profissional.

O guia definitivo de IN, ON e AT — e muito mais!

As 3 Grandes Preposições

📦 IN — dentro de um espaço fechado → in a box, in the room, in Brazil
📋 ON — em cima de uma superfície → on the table, on the wall, on the floor
📍 AT — em um ponto específico → at the door, at school, at 5pm

Use IN para espaços que cercam algo por todos os lados:

📦 The book is in the bag. / She lives in Brazil.

🏙️ He is in the city / in São Paulo / in the kitchen.

🌊 The fish swims in the water. / The sun is in the sky.

Use ON para superfícies (mesa, parede, chão) e linhas (rua, andar):

🪑 The cup is on the table. / The picture is on the wall.

🏘️ She lives on Rua das Flores. / I'm on the second floor.

📺 It's on TV. / He is on the bus / plane / ship.

Use AT para um ponto específico no espaço ou lugares como atividade:

🚪 She is at the door. / Meet me at the corner.

🏫 He is at school / at work / at the airport.

⚡ I'm at home. / She arrived at the station.

Além de IN, ON e AT:

under — embaixo de

over / above — acima de

behind — atrás de

in front of — na frente de

next to / beside — ao lado de

between — entre (2)

among — entre (3+)

near — perto de

opposite — em frente a / oposto

inside/outside — dentro/fora

Cuidado com as diferenças do português!

✅ at home — em casa (não: in home)

✅ in the morning — de manhã (tempo, não lugar)

✅ on the bus / train / plane — no ônibus/trem/avião

✅ at school / at work — na escola / no trabalho (atividade)

🎭 Dialogue

🗺️ Mapa de Nano Banana. Leia em voz alta.

🗺️ Mapa de Nano Banana. Yara ajuda Leo a encontrar lugares na ilha usando o mapa gigante da praça central!

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo, are you lost? Come look at this map. We're standing right here — in front of the central square.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Yes! I was looking for the library. Someone told me it was next to the park, but I can't find it!

🐾 Capy Yara

Ah! It's behind the big tree, between the bakery and the school. It's on Capybara Street.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Oh! Is the school the one opposite the river? I walked under a big wooden bridge to get there.

🐾 Capy Yara

Exactly! And the park is near the river, with benches among the trees. So beautiful in the morning!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Got it! So the library is at the end of the street, inside that yellow building, right? I'm on my way! 🗺️

Grammar: Virtual Meeting Language

Estruturas gramaticais para reuniões virtuais e comunicação remota profissional.

"We're meeting tomorrow at 10 to review the deliverables."
"She's joining the call from a different time zone today."
"They're presenting the stand-up results this afternoon."
💡 Regra: Present Continuous para eventos futuros já agendados — indica que algo está planejado e confirmado.
"Sorry to interrupt, but I think there's some background noise."
"Could I just jump in here and add something?"
"If I may, I'd like to raise a point about the workload."
⚡ Dica: Interrupções educadas são essenciais em videochamadas — nunca interrompa sem sinalizar que quer falar.
"Does that make sense? Any questions before we move on?"
"Are we aligned on this deliverable before the stand-up tomorrow?"
"Can everyone confirm they received the async update?"
🗣️ Dica: Confirmar entendimento é crítico em reuniões remotas — problemas de conexão e fusos horários aumentam chances de mal-entendidos.

Grammar: Present Perfect

Four key rules for using Present Perfect correctly.

Regular past participles: watch→watched, discover→discovered, achieve→achieved

Irregular: go→gone, see→seen, write→written, take→taken, make→made, find→found

"I have watched all of Friends." (I/you/we/they)
"She has seen every GoT episode." (he/she/it)
EVER = at any point in your life: "Have you ever watched Friends?"
NEVER = not at any point: "I have never seen GoT."
Position: EVER and NEVER go between have/has and the past participle.
JUST = very recently: "I've just finished watching it."
ALREADY = before expected: "I've already seen it twice!"
YET = in negatives/questions: "I haven't watched it yet." / "Have you seen it yet?"
FOR = duration: "I've studied English for two years."
SINCE = starting point: "I've lived here since 2020."

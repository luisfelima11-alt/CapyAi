📐 Resumo Prático das 20 Aulas (B1 → B2)

Use estes 4 blocos em aula para revisar rapidamente todas as situações do curso antes de abrir a prova.

ON (Dias da semana e Datas específicas): on Monday, on Tuesdays, on Friday afternoon, on Saturday night, on July 15th. ⚠️ Nunca diga in Monday, at Monday ou on the Monday!
IN (Meses, Anos, Partes do dia e "Daqui a..."): in July, in 2023, in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, in five years (daqui a 5 anos).
AT (Horários e Noite): at 7 p.m., at 3 o'clock, at noon, at midnight, at night.
Transporte & Modo: by bus, by car, by subway, by plane, mas on foot (a pé). E lembre: by accident (sem querer) vs. on purpose (de propósito) vs. on my own (sozinho).
Ação em andamento + interrupção: O que estava rolando (While I was cooking dinner...) + o que interrompeu (...the lights went out).
Experiência de vida vs. Quando aconteceu: Experiência sem data = I have been to London three times. Com data marcada = My last trip was in 2022.
FOR vs. SINCE: for three years (tempo de duração) vs. since 2021 / since we got married (quando começou).
Algo que já tinha acontecido (had + ação): Muito usado com By the time: "By the time we arrived, the movie had already started."
Much / Many / Some / Any: Com tempo/dinheiro na negativa usamos much (don't have much time); negativas e perguntas no plural usam any (haven't packed any bags).
A / An / The: Profissões sempre levam a/an (she is an architect). Ideias gerais entram direto sem "the" (creativity is essential).
"Foi construído / Foi escrito": "The bridge was built 100 years ago" / "Was this report written by marketing?"
To + ação vs. Ação com -ING: Depois de decide, want, plan, hope usamos to + ação (decided to start). Depois de enjoy, avoid, finish e depois de interested in, good at, look forward to usamos -ING (enjoys running, looking forward to seeing).
Used to ("Eu costumava..."): "I used to be shy" / "I used to drink four cups of coffee."
Wish I had... ("Queria ter feito..."): "I wish I had practised harder" / "I wish I had started earlier."
Must have... ("Deve ter acontecido"): "He isn't answering his phone. He must have left already."
Confirmação no final da frase: "You have finished, haven't you?" / "You are coming, aren't you?"
Meta futura & Situação imaginária: "By this time next year, I will have completed my course" + "If I knew I couldn't fail, I would start my own business."

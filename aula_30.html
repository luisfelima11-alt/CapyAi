Grammar: Making Plans

Key structures for inviting, accepting, declining, and suggesting.

"Would you like to watch Friends tonight?"
"How about we grab coffee this weekend?"
"Do you want to hang out on Saturday?"
"What if we watched Game of Thrones together?"
"I'd love to! What time?"
"Count me in! I've been waiting for this."
"That sounds amazing! I'm definitely in."
"Absolutely! I'll be there."
"I'd love to, but I already have plans."
"I'm afraid I can't make it tonight."
"Maybe another time? I have to work late."
"I have to pass this time, but let's plan something soon."
"What about Sunday instead? I'm free all day."
"Could we reschedule for next week?"
"How about we try a different day? Thursday works for me."

Grammar: Going to + GET Phrasal Verbs

Planos decididos e os 5 phrasal verbs essenciais com GET.

"I am going to start a new routine." → Plano decidido antes.
"She is going to prepare the presentation."
"We are going to decide together."
⚠️ Going to vs Will:
Going to → plano JÁ decidido antes: "I'm going to buy a phone."
Will → decisão espontânea agora: "I'll help you!"
✅ Fala: "I'm gonna start the project tomorrow."
✅ Escrita formal: "I am going to start the project tomorrow."
❌ NUNCA escreva "gonna" em e-mails profissionais ou redações!
GET UP= levantar (da cama/cadeira) → "I get up at 7am every day."
GET ON= entrar em / embarcar → "She got on the bus at 8am."
GET OFF= sair / desembarcar → "He got off the train downtown."
GET OUT= sair de um lugar/situação → "Get out of bed! It's late!"
GET IN= entrar no carro → "She got in the car and drove away."
🚌 Dica: GET ON/OFF = transporte público (bus, train, plane) | GET IN/OUT = carro/táxi

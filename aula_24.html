Special Uses of TO HAVE

In restaurant and daily life contexts, TO HAVE has special meanings beyond "possessing" something.

📌 TO HAVE — 4 Key Uses in Context

Ordering
"I'll have the pasta"
Eating/Drinking
"We're having dinner"
Experience
"Have you tried sushi?"
Invitation
"Have some cake!"
"I'll have the grilled salmon, please." (ordering)
"What will you have?" — "I'll have the steak."
"I'd like a glass of water, please." (more formal)

📝 "I'll have" = "Vou querer..." — the most natural way to order in English!

"We're having lunch right now." (eating)
"I had coffee this morning." (drank)
"Let's have dinner together tonight." (eat)
"Have you ever had Brazilian churrasco?" (experience)
"I've never had sushi before." (first time)
"You should have the special — it's amazing!" (recommendation)

🍽️ Useful Restaurant Phrases

Ordering

"I'll have the chicken, please."

Asking for the bill

"Could I have the bill, please?"

Asking for a recommendation

"What do you recommend?"

Dietary needs

"Is there anything vegetarian?"

Complimenting the food

"This is delicious! My compliments to the chef."

Splitting the bill

"Can we have separate bills?"

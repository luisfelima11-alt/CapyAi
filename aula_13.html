📐 Grammar

Collocations de trabalho: DO, MAKE e HAVE.

A Regra das Collocations

✅ DO a task / a report / your job / research / your best
✅ MAKE a decision / a plan / a mistake / a presentation / money
✅ HAVE a meeting / a deadline / a break / an interview / experience

Como usar WORK com preposições e advérbios:

work hard — trabalhar duro / trabalhar muito

work from home — trabalhar de casa

work overtime — fazer hora extra

work as a (teacher/manager) — trabalhar como...

work for (a company) — trabalhar para...

work with (a team) — trabalhar com...

A distinção mais confusa para brasileiros!

DO: do a task, do research, do your best, do the work, do overtime

MAKE: make a decision, make a plan, make a mistake, make a presentation, make money

Dica: DO = processo / ação rotineira. MAKE = criar / resultado.

Present Simple para rotinas profissionais:

I work from 9 to 6. / She manages a team of 10.

We have weekly meetings on Mondays.

He reports to the CEO. / I deal with customer complaints.

Frases essenciais para uma entrevista de emprego:

"What are your skills?" / "I have 3 years of experience."

"I work well under pressure." / "I'm a team player."

"My goal is to grow with the company." / "I'm very motivated."

"I can start immediately." / "I'm looking for new challenges."

🖥️ Escritório de Nano Banana. Leo tem sua primeira entrevista de emprego na empresa de Yara!

🐾 Capy Yara (interviewer)

Welcome, Leo! Please sit down. Tell me — what experience do you have, and what are your main skills?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Thank you! I've been working as an English student for 3 months. I work hard, I'm good at teamwork, and I always meet deadlines.

🐾 Capy Yara

Impressive! Can you work from home? And are you comfortable making presentations to large groups?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Yes! I work from home three days a week. And I love doing presentations — I work well under pressure and I never make the same mistake twice.

🐾 Capy Yara

One last question — how important is work-life balance to you? We don't want people working overtime every day here.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I couldn't agree more! I believe a good work-life balance makes you more productive. My goal is to grow with this company and do my best every day! 🤝

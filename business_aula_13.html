Grammar: Data & Trends Language

Estruturas gramaticais para apresentar dados, tendências e análises em inglês profissional.

"The conversion rate rose by 15% in Q3 compared to last quarter."
"Churn fell significantly after we implemented the new onboarding flow."
"Revenue remained stable despite the market fluctuations."
💡 Verbos de tendência: rise/rose, fall/fell, grow/grew, drop/dropped, remain/remained, stabilize/stabilized + by (%) ou to (valor absoluto).
"We missed our Q3 target by 12% — a significant shortfall."
"One in three users churned within the first 30 days."
"Growth doubled after implementing the dashboard KPIs."
⚡ Dica: "By" indica mudança relativa (by 12%), "to" indica valor final (to $2M). "Doubled" = 100% increase, "halved" = 50% decrease.
"The data was collected over a 12-month period."
"The metrics were analyzed and the dashboard was updated."
"The forecast was calculated using Q3 benchmarks."
🗣️ Regra: Passive voice (was/were + past participle) é muito comum em contextos de dados — o foco é no processo, não em quem fez.

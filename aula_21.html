Comparative Adjectives

Three rules to compare two things in English.

📌 Structure

Subject + verb + comparative adjective + than + object

Short adjectives
fast → faster
Long adjectives
more expensive
Irregular
good → better
tall → taller than  |  fast → faster than
big → bigger than  |  happy → happier than
cheap → cheaper than  |  healthy → healthier than

📝 Double the final consonant if the adjective ends in consonant-vowel-consonant: big → bigger, hot → hotter

This phone is more expensive than the other one.
Spanish is less difficult than Mandarin for English speakers.
This job is more interesting than my last one.

📝 Never say more faster or more bigger — choose one rule only!

🔍 Quick Spelling Rules

-e endings: just add -r   nice → nicer
-y endings: change to -ier   busy → busier
CVC pattern: double consonant   hot → hotter
2 syllables: either form works   clever → cleverer / more clever

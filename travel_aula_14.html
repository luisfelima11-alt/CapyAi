Grammar: Banking English

Voz passiva, verbos modais para pedidos e números em inglês.

"The money will be transferred within three business days."
"Your card was declined due to insufficient funds."
"A fee is charged for international transactions."
📐 Regra: Passiva = to be + past participle. Muito usada em avisos e comunicações bancárias formais.
"I'd like to withdraw €200, please." → Pedido educado.
"Could I exchange these dollars for euros?" → Pergunta formal.
"I need to check my balance before making a withdrawal." → Necessidade.
"Would it be possible to waive the transaction fee?" → Pedido muito formal.
€1,500 → "one thousand five hundred euros"
2.5% → "two point five percent"
$10,000 → "ten thousand dollars"
£50.75 → "fifty pounds and seventy-five pence"

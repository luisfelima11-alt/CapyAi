📐 Grammar

Como usar verbos irregulares no passado.

A Regra de Ouro

✅ Afirmativa: sujeito + forma irregular → I went / She said
❌ Negativa: sujeito + didn't + verbo BASE → I didn't go
❓ Pergunta: Did + sujeito + verbo BASE? → Did she say?

São os verbos mais antigos e mais usados do inglês — e cada um mudou de forma própria ao longo dos séculos!

Regular: walk → walked (apenas +ED)

Irregular: go → went (forma completamente diferente!)

A boa notícia: são só ~150 verbos irregulares comuns — e você já usa vários no dia a dia!

A forma muda completamente:

go → went

say → said

see → saw

come → came

think → thought

give → gave

make → made

get → got

find → found

know → knew

Mesma forma no presente e no passado — atenção ao contexto!

put → put | cut → cut | hit → hit

read → read (pronuncia-se diferente: "red"!) | let → let

Com didn't e Did, sempre use o verbo no infinitivo — nunca a forma irregular!

✅ I didn't go (não: I didn't went)

✅ Did you see her? (não: Did you saw her?)

✅ He didn't know → Ele não sabia

Memorize estes primeiro!

1. be → was/were

2. have → had

3. go → went

4. do → did

5. say → said

6. get → got

7. make → made

8. know → knew

9. think → thought

10. see → saw

🏛️ Museu de Nano Banana. Leo e Yara visitam o museu histórico da ilha e encontram artefatos do passado!

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo! Did you know this museum has artifacts from ancient Nano Banana? Look at this — someone made this over 500 years ago!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

Wow! I had no idea! I came here thinking it was a small place, but it's huge! How did you find out about it?

🐾 Capy Yara

A local told me! They said the first settlers built everything by hand — no machines. They knew exactly what they were doing.

🧑‍🦱 Leo

That's incredible. I saw a painting over there — it showed capybaras and humans together. They really got along well!

🐾 Capy Yara

Always! And did you know that the first capybara here taught the settlers to swim? She went into the river and everyone followed!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I didn't know that! I thought capybaras were always just friendly, but they were teachers too! Just like you, Yara! 🌟

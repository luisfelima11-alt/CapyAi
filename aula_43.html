3. Error Correction

Find the mistake and rewrite the sentence correctly.

❌ "Would you like come to Rio with us?"

💡 Hint: would like come → would like to come

❌ "How about we to fly on Friday morning?"

💡 Hint: How about we to fly → How about we fly (no "to")

❌ "She gonna to visit Rio next month."

💡 Hint: gonna to → is going to (correct form: subject + is/are + going to + verb)

Grammar: Transport English

Should/Must, preposições de movimento e pedidos de ajuda.

"You should validate your ticket before boarding." → Conselho/recomendação.
"You must tap in and out with your card." → Regra obrigatória.
"You shouldn't block the doors during rush hour." → Proibição leve.
📐 Regra: Must = obrigação forte (regra); Should = recomendação ou conselho.
"Get on the bus at the next stop." → Embarcar (ônibus/trem/metrô).
"Get off at Central Station." → Desembarcar.
"Get into the taxi." → Entrar (veículo fechado pequeno).
"Get out of the car." → Sair (veículo fechado pequeno).
"Excuse me, could you tell me which line goes to the airport?"
"Would you mind telling me where to transfer?"
"I'm sorry to bother you, but is this the right platform?"
"Could you help me figure out which bus to take?"

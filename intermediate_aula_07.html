Grammar: Many, Much & A Lot Of

Um ponto só: o que dá pra contar e o que não dá. É o que decide entre many e much.

ZERO: if + present, present → fatos gerais → "If you heat ice, it melts."
FIRST: if + present, will + infinitive → possibilidade real → "If it rains, I will stay home."
SECOND: if + past, would + infinitive → hipotético / improvável → "If I won the lottery, I would travel."
THIRD: if + past perfect, would have + past participle → passado impossível → "If I had studied, I would have passed."
📐 Sinais: sempre verdadeiro = Zero · pode acontecer = First · imaginário agora = Second · arrependimento do passado = Third.
Nunca 'will' na if-clause: "If I will study" ❌ → "If I study, I will pass." ✅
Subjuntivo 'were': "If I were you, I would accept the offer." (sempre 'were', não 'was').
Third = would HAVE + pp: "If I had known, I would have called." (não "would called").
⚠️ Erro comum: "If I would study more, I will pass" ❌ → "If I study more, I will pass" ✅ (nunca 'will/would' na oração com if).
Condição passada com resultado presente: "If I had taken that job, I would be living in London now." (Past Perfect na if-clause + would + infinitive no presente).
"If she had studied medicine, she would be a doctor today."
📐 Dica: condicionais mistas conectam um passado impossível com a sua consequência ainda visível hoje. Combine Third (if-clause) + Second (resultado).

Grammar Rules

3 key rules for describing personality and mood in English.

Use to be when describing someone's permanent personality.

She is shy. — always, naturally
He is very ambitious. — a core trait
They are incredibly creative. — who they are

Use to feel or to seem when describing temporary emotional states.

She feels nervous. — right now
He seems tired today. — at this moment
I felt overwhelmed yesterday. — past state

Use to act when describing someone's current behavior (which may differ from their personality).

He is acting strange. — unusual behavior
Stop acting stubborn! — in this moment
She acted confident at the interview. — on that occasion

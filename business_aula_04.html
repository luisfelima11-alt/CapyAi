Grammar: Presentation Language

Estruturas para apresentar dados, descrever tendências e organizar sua fala.

"The chart shows a 15% increase in Q3." — fato (Simple Present)
"Sales are growing steadily." — tendência em curso (Present Continuous)
"As you can see, the trend is positive." — referir ao slide
📈 "Sales rose by 20% compared to last year."
📉 "Costs fell significantly in Q4."
➡️ "Revenue remained stable throughout the year."
📊 Adverbs: rose sharply / slightly / dramatically / steadily — modifique a intensidade da mudança.
"Firstly, I'd like to give you an overview of our results."
"Moving on, let's look at the key takeaways."
"Finally, I'd like to draw your attention to the Q&A."
"In conclusion, the data supports our strategy."

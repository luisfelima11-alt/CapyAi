Verbos de Percepção e Ação

COME ➡️ THINK 💭 LOOK 👀 GIVE 🎁

I/You/We/They come | He/She/It comes

✅ Come on! → Vamos! / Vai! / Vem cá!

✅ She comes from Brazil. → Ela é do Brasil.

✅ Dreams come true. → Sonhos se realizam.

I/You/We/They think | He/She/It thinks

✅ I think so. → Acho que sim.

✅ What do you think? → O que você acha?

✅ Think positive! → Pense positivo!

I/You/We/They look | He/She/It looks

✅ Look at this! → Olha isso!

✅ You look great today! → Você está ótimo hoje!

✅ It looks difficult but it's easy. → Parece difícil mas é fácil.

💡 LOOK = intencional. SEE = automático. WATCH = por tempo.

I/You/We/They give | He/She/It gives

✅ Give it a shot! → Tenta aí! / Arrisca!

✅ She gives great advice. → Ela dá ótimos conselhos.

✅ Give me a minute. → Me dá um minuto.

🌻 Jardim de Nano Banana. Leo e Yara exploram o jardim mágico e Leo aprende a expressar opiniões e percepções.

🐾 Capy Yara

Leo, look at these flowers! What do you think?

🧑‍🦱 Leo

They look amazing! I think this is the most beautiful garden I've ever seen. Where do the seeds come from?

🐾 Capy Yara

They come from all over the world! I give them water and love every day. Come on, I'll show you the rare ones!

🧑‍🦱 Leo

I think I want to grow a garden too! Can I give it a shot?

🐾 Capy Yara

Of course! Just give it time and attention. It looks hard but it's simple. Think positive and the flowers will come! 🌸

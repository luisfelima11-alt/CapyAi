Grammar: Networking English

Apresentações profissionais, elevator pitch e follow-up após eventos.

"Hi, I'm [name]. I work in [sector] at [company]."
"I'm a [role] specialising in [area]."
"I'm here representing [company] — we work on [topic]."
📐 Tip: Keep your intro to 2 sentences — then ask THEM a question.
Problem → Solution → Result.
"We help [target] to [solve problem] by [your approach], which means [key benefit]."
Example: "We help small businesses to manage their finances by automating bookkeeping, which means they save 10 hours a week."
"It was great to meet you at [event]."
"As promised, I'm sending over the information we discussed."
"I'd love to continue our conversation about [topic]."
"I thought of you when I saw this article — [link]."
📐 Tip: Follow up within 24–48 hours while the conversation is fresh.

Grammar: Talking About Movies & TV

Key structures for opinions, recommendations, descriptions and comparisons.

"In my opinion, Friends has the best writing in sitcom history."
"Personally, I think House of the Dragon is more intense than GoT."
"To be honest, I prefer series to movies."
"If you like supernatural stories, you'll love Lucifer."
"I'd recommend watching 'Up' — it's extraordinary."
"You should definitely watch Benjamin Button."
"Lucifer is about a devil who moves to LA and becomes a detective."
"It follows a teenager who accidentally travels through time."
"The story revolves around a group of friends in New York."
"While Friends is a comedy, Elite is a dark thriller."
"Compared to GoT, House of the Dragon has a tighter plot."

Grammar: Entertainment English

Futuros, disponibilidade e preços, convites e sugestões.

"I am going to see the show tonight." → Plano já decidido.
"I will book two tickets right now." → Decisão espontânea no momento.
"The band is going to perform three encores." → Previsão baseada em plano.
📐 Regra: "Going to" = plano pré-existente. "Will" = decisão ou promessa no momento da fala.
"Do you have any tickets left for tonight?"
"How much is the cover charge?"
"Are there any seats available near the front?"
"Is there a discount for students or groups?"
"Why don't we check out that jazz bar tonight?" → Sugestão informal.
"How about getting tickets for the comedy show?" → Proposta suave.
"Let's go to the after-party — it sounds amazing!" → Convite direto.
"Shall we make a reservation for the restaurant first?" → Sugestão mais formal.

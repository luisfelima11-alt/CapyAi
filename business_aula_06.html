Grammar: Negotiation Language

Estruturas gramaticais para negociações profissionais em inglês.

"If you lower the price, we'll increase the order quantity."
"If we can agree on the deadline, we'll sign off today."
"If you extend the warranty, we'll drop the counteroffer."
💡 Regra: First Conditional: If + Present Simple → will + base verb. Perfeito para propostas condicionais.
"I think we can work something out." — suaviza a posição
"I believe our proposal is very competitive." — expressa opinião profissional
"It seems like this could be a win-win situation." — tom neutro
⚡ Dica: Hedging suaviza posições e torna a linguagem mais diplomática — essencial em negociações.
"Could you extend the payment terms to 60 days?"
"Would you consider a three-month pilot program?"
"We could offer a 10% discount if you commit by Friday."
🗣️ Dica: Could/Would tornam pedidos e ofertas mais educados e profissionais do que usando o imperativo.

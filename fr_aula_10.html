How to ask for and give directions in French. Click to expand.

📐 Grammar

Asking the way, giving orders politely, and placing things in space.

Direction Formula

Pour aller à [lieu], + imperative...

Examples

Tournez à gauche → Turn left

Continuez tout droit → Go straight

C'est en face de la banque → It's across from the bank

Contractions

à + le = au (au coin)

à + les = aux

de + le = du (près du parc)

Three polite ways to ask the way / Três formas educadas de perguntar o caminho:

✅ Où est la gare? — Where is the station? / Onde fica a estação?

✅ Pour aller à la gare? — How do I get to the station? / Como chego à estação?

✅ Pardon, je cherche la gare. — Excuse me, I'm looking for the station. / Com licença, procuro a estação.

💡 Always start with "Pardon" (excuse me) — politeness is essential in French! / Sempre comece com "Pardon".

To give directions politely, use the vous imperative — just drop "vous" / Para dar direções, use o imperativo com "vous" (sem o "vous"):

✅ Tournez à droite. — Turn right. / Vire à direita.

✅ Continuez tout droit. — Continue straight. / Continue em frente.

✅ Allez jusqu'au feu. — Go up to the light. / Vá até o sinal.

✅ Traversez la rue. — Cross the street. / Atravesse a rua.

✅ Prenez la première rue à gauche. — Take the first street on the left. / Pegue a primeira rua à esquerda.

These prepositions tell you where something is / Estas preposições dizem onde algo está:

✅ en face de — across from / em frente a

✅ à côté de — next to / ao lado de

✅ près de — near / perto de

✅ loin de — far from / longe de

✅ au coin de — at the corner of / na esquina de

💡 With "le", these contract! à + le = au, de + le = du. So: "au coin du parc" (at the corner of the park), "en face du musée" (across from the museum). / Com "le", contraem: à+le=au, de+le=du.

🎭 Dialogue

🧭 Yara is lost and asks for the way to the station. Read aloud!

🧭 Yara is lost in the city and stops a passer-by (un passant) to find the train station — listen for the directions and prepositions!

🐾 Yara

Pardon, Monsieur! Je suis perdue. Pour aller à la gare, s'il vous plaît?

Excuse me, Sir! I'm lost. How do I get to the station, please?

🧑‍🦱 Le passant

La gare? C'est facile. Continuez tout droit jusqu'au feu rouge.

The station? It's easy. Go straight ahead until the red light.

🐾 Yara

D'accord, tout droit jusqu'au feu. Et après?

Okay, straight ahead to the light. And then?

🧑‍🦱 Le passant

Au feu, tournez à droite. Puis prenez la première rue à gauche.

At the light, turn right. Then take the first street on the left.

🐾 Yara

Et la gare, elle est où exactement?

And the station, where is it exactly?

🧑‍🦱 Le passant

La gare est en face de la banque, à côté du café. C'est tout près!

The station is across from the bank, next to the café. It's very close!

🐾 Yara

Merci beaucoup, Monsieur! Vous êtes très gentil. Au revoir!

Thank you very much, Sir! You're very kind. Goodbye!

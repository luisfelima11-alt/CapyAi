Grammar: Professional Complaint English

Passive Voice, Conditional Type 2 e advérbios profissionais.

"Your complaint has been received." → Voz passiva remove responsabilidade pessoal.
"The issue will be investigated." → Tom profissional e institucional.
"A refund has been processed." → Foco na ação, não em quem agiu.
📐 Rule: be + past participle removes personal blame and sounds more professional.
"If you could provide your order number, we'd be able to help." → Pedido educado.
"If there's anything else I can do, please let me know." → Oferta de ajuda.
📐 Rule: if + past simple → would + base verb for polite professional language.
"I sincerely apologize." → Peso emocional e profissional.
"We fully understand your frustration." → Validação total.
"We will immediately investigate." → Urgência e comprometimento.
📐 Rule: adverbs before verbs or after "to be" to add professional weight.

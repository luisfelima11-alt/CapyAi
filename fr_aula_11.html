📐 Grammar

The verb "porter", pointing at things, and making colours agree.

Verb: porter (to wear)

Je porte un pull bleu.

Singular

je porte — I wear

tu portes — you wear

il/elle porte — he/she wears

Plural

nous portons — we wear

vous portez — you wear

ils/elles portent — they wear

Porter is a regular -ER verb. It means "to wear" (and also "to carry") / Porter é um verbo regular em -ER. Significa "vestir/usar":

✅ je porte, tu portes, il/elle porte

✅ nous portons, vous portez, ils/elles portent

✅ Aujourd'hui, je porte une robe rouge. — Today I'm wearing a red dress. / Hoje uso um vestido vermelho.

💡 The endings -e, -es, -e, -ent are silent — porte, portes, portent all sound the same! / As terminações são mudas.

To say "this/that/these", the word changes with gender and number / Para dizer "este/esta/estes", a palavra muda com gênero e número:

✅ ce pull (masculine) — this sweater / este suéter

✅ cette robe (feminine) — this dress / este vestido

✅ cet imperméable (masculine before a vowel/h) — this raincoat / esta capa de chuva

✅ ces chaussures (plural) — these shoes / estes sapatos

💡 Use "cet" only for masculine words starting with a vowel or silent h: cet homme, cet anorak. / Use "cet" só antes de vogal.

Colours (and most adjectives) agree with the noun in gender and number / As cores concordam com o substantivo em gênero e número:

✅ un pull bleu (masc.) → une robe bleue (fem. = add -e)

✅ des chaussures noires (fem. plural = add -es)

✅ un manteau vert → une jupe verte → des pulls verts

💡 Feminine = +e, plural = +s. Some colours never change: orange, marron stay the same! / Feminino = +e, plural = +s. "orange" e "marron" não mudam.

🎭 Dialogue

🛍️ Yara goes clothes shopping. Read aloud!

🛍️ Yara is in a clothes shop and talks to a sales assistant (une vendeuse) — listen for the size, the price, and trying things on!

🧑‍🦱 La vendeuse

Bonjour! Je peux vous aider?

Hello! Can I help you?

🐾 Yara

Bonjour! Oui, je cherche une robe pour une fête.

Hello! Yes, I'm looking for a dress for a party.

🧑‍🦱 La vendeuse

Très bien. Quelle taille faites-vous? Cette robe bleue est très jolie.

Very good. What size are you? This blue dress is very pretty.

🐾 Yara

Je fais du 38. Je peux l'essayer?

I'm a size 38. Can I try it on?

🧑‍🦱 La vendeuse

Bien sûr! La cabine est là-bas. ... Elle vous va très bien!

Of course! The fitting room is over there. ... It suits you very well!

🐾 Yara

J'adore! C'est combien?

I love it! How much is it?

🧑‍🦱 La vendeuse

Elle coûte 45 euros. Ce n'est pas cher!

It costs 45 euros. It's not expensive!

🐾 Yara

Parfait, je la prends! Merci beaucoup!

Perfect, I'll take it! Thank you very much!

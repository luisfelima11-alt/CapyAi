WILL — 4 Key Uses

WILL is the most versatile future form. Each use has a different meaning and context.

📌 Structure

Subject + will + base verb  |  Subject + won't + base verb

✔ Affirmative
I will go
✘ Negative
She won't come
? Question
Will they help?
"I think it will rain tomorrow."
"Technology will change the world."
"She will probably get the job."

📝 Common with: I think, I believe, I expect, probably, maybe

"I will call you tomorrow, I promise."
"I won't forget to send the email."
"We will always support you."

📝 Often heard with: I promise, I swear, don't worry, I won't let you down

"The phone is ringing!" — "I 'll get it!" (decided now)
"It's hot in here." — "I 'll open the window."
"I 'll have the pasta." (ordering at restaurant)

📝 Contrast: "I'm going to call" = planned in advance | "I'll call" = decided right now

"Will you help me with this?" (request)
"I 'll carry your bags." (offer)
"Will you please be quiet?" (request)

Grammar: Onboarding Language

Estruturas gramaticais para os seus primeiros dias em uma empresa.

"We're delighted to welcome you to the team, Yara!"
"Feel free to reach out if you have any questions."
"Don't hesitate to ask — that's what we're here for."
💡 Dica: Welcoming phrases usam verbos como feel free, don't hesitate e we're glad — expressam receptividade e abertura.
"Could you show me how the system works?"
"Where can I find the employee handbook?"
"Who should I contact about the dress code?"
⚡ Dica: Use "Could you show me" e "Where can I find" para perguntas educadas — muito mais profissional do que simplesmente "Where is X?"
"We start at 9am and the core hours run until 5pm."
"The team meets every Monday for a stand-up."
"The company offers flexible benefits after your probation."
🗣️ Regra: Present Simple descreve rotinas, horários e políticas fixas da empresa — fatos permanentes, não eventos pontuais.

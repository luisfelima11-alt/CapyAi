# Ilustrações — histórias intermediárias

Direção visual: ilustração 3D de animação fofa, estilo Pixar; personagens recorrentes, luz cinematográfica suave, cores quentes com detalhes teal e navy. Composição horizontal 3:2. Sem texto, letras, números, logotipos ou marcas-d’água. A imagem é a mesma nos quatro idiomas; o texto e as traduções ficam no leitor, nunca dentro da arte.

Saída: `assets/stories/station-1.webp` a `station-8.webp` e `garden-1.webp` a `garden-4.webp`, largura 900 px. Imagens originais geradas com a ferramenta de imagens e otimizadas em WebP.

## station

Personagens: Maya, a young adult woman with brown skin, dark braided hair in a high bun, bright mustard raincoat, teal backpack; an older gray-haired woman with glasses in a plum coat; when present a young adult male station worker in a navy jacket.

### station-1.webp

Maya steps off a small commuter train at a seaside station during heavy rain, clutching the edge of her mustard raincoat, teal backpack visible, the platform almost empty.

### station-2.webp

Under a rain-streaked canopy, Maya watches an elderly woman in a plum coat anxiously searching through her bag on a bench, the empty spot beside her and her wet shoes visible.

### station-3.webp

Maya and the elderly woman stand by a small weathered station ticket booth discussing the missing item with a kind young station worker, windows wet from rain, no signs or printed material anywhere.

### station-4.webp

Maya searches a quiet carriage with the station worker and peers under a wooden seat beside the train door; the yellow raincoat and teal backpack stand out.

### station-5.webp

Maya holds up a small worn red leather case from the gap between the seat and wall, her relieved expression lit by amber overhead lights; worker in navy jacket nearby.

### station-6.webp

Back on the platform Maya returns the red leather case to the older woman, who opens it and looks with emotion at one faded photograph of two young adults by the sea, no text anywhere.

### station-7.webp

The rain has eased at the station. Maya and the gray-haired woman sit together on the platform bench, drinking from plain takeaway cups and smiling while sunlight appears across puddles.

### station-8.webp

Maya walks away from the seaside station towards a distant bicycle shop on a clear wet street, smiling and waving back to the elderly woman by the platform, amber sunlight, quiet hopeful ending.

## garden

Personagens: two neighbors: a young South Asian woman with black bob haircut and a dark teal work apron over a cream shirt, and a middle-aged Black man in rust-orange sweater, optionally a small group of residents.

### garden-1.webp

On a rooftop terrace above a residential neighborhood, the two neighbors look at rows of dry seedlings under a harsh afternoon sun, a simple empty rain barrel and clay pots nearby.

### garden-2.webp

The two neighbors and a small group of apartment residents gather around a wooden table on the rooftop in an uneasy discussion, a cracked empty flowerpot and plain unmarked watering can on the table.

### garden-3.webp

The two neighbors work together to move lightweight plant pots into cool shade and add straw mulch around their soil, amber and teal clothing consistent, late afternoon sunlight.

### garden-4.webp

On the successful leafy rooftop garden at dusk, several neighbors including both leads share a small bowl of ripe tomatoes, smiling near the restored planters, city skyline and pink sunset behind.

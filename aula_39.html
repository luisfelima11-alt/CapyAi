📐 Grammar

A estrutura do 2nd Conditional — o condicional dos sonhos.

The 2nd Conditional Formula

IF + Past Simple → WOULD + base verb

✅ If I had a million dollars, I would travel the world.
✅ If she were taller, she would be a model.
❌ If I would have money, I travel. (WRONG)
